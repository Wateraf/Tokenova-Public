# Tokenova Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

Tokenova ("the App") is published by mizulab. This policy explains what data the App uses and why.

## Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device:

- Wallet balances (tickets, gold coins, rainbow coins)
- Daily claim, timed claim, and ad-view counts
- Letter lights, jackpot board, dragon collection, and book rewards
- Sound and haptic preferences
- Monthly pass claim state (purchase status is verified via Apple)

This information stays on your device unless you delete the App.

## Advertising

The App may show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data to serve ads, in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Watching rewarded ads is optional. There are no forced interstitial ads.

## In-app purchases

Rainbow coin packs and the 30 Days Pass are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
