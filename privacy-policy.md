# Tokenova Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

Tokenova ("the App") is published by mizulab. This policy explains what data the App uses and why.

## Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device:

- Wallet balances (tickets, gold coins, rainbow coins)
- Daily claim, timed claim, and ad-view counts
- Letter lights, jackpot board, dragon collection, and book rewards
- Sound and haptic preferences
- 30 Days Pass claim state (purchase status is verified via Apple)

You can play without creating an account. Guest progress stays on the device unless you delete the App or the save.

## Account and cloud (optional)

Sign in with Apple is optional (Settings → Account). If you sign in, we create a cloud account so wallet balances and claim state can come back after reinstall or a new iPhone.

That account uses:

- **User ID** — a random account id (the Player ID in Settings → Account). Used only to load and save your cloud wallet.
- **Email address** — if Apple provides an email with Sign in with Apple, it is stored on the account for sign-in. We do not use it for marketing.

Letter lights, the jackpot board, and dragon collection stay on the device. They are not the cloud save.

Delete Account (Settings → Account) erases the cloud save. It cannot be undone.

## Advertising

The App may show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising data to serve ads, in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency or use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. Watching rewarded ads is optional. There are no forced interstitial ads.

## In-app purchases

Rainbow coin packs and the 30 Days Pass are processed by Apple. We keep a record that a pack or pass was granted to your account so it can be restored. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
