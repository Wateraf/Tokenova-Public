# Tokenova Support

## Quick help

| Topic | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Drop coins** | Center star or spacebar. Aim with the side buttons or A / D. |
| **Tickets** | D / R / A +5, G / N +30, O +50. Bottom miss +1. JACKPOT pays the board. |
| **Exchange** | 15 tickets → 1 gold coin. Gold coins cap at 1000. Rainbow coins are uncapped. |
| **Daily / timed** | Settings is not required — use the gift icon. Login once per local day. Timed claims every 10 minutes, up to 5 per day. |
| **Ads** | Optional rewarded ads: 4× gold (10/day) and regular gold (20/day). |
| **Monthly Pass** | One tap to claim remaining timed and 4× ad gold for the day. Manage subscription in your Apple ID settings. |
| **Sound / haptics** | Settings → Music / SFX sliders and Vibration |

## Feedback & bug reports

The best way to reach us is an **App Store review** for Tokenova:

1. Open **Settings** in the App → **About** → **Feedback & Bug Reports**, or
2. Find Tokenova on the App Store → **Write a Review**

Reviews help us prioritize fixes and improvements.

## Other issues

For account or billing problems with purchases, contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing) — Apple processes all payments.

For anything else, leave an App Store review or open an issue on [Tokenova-Public](https://github.com/Wateraf/Tokenova-Public/issues).
