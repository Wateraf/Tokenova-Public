## Common topics

**Lost data / new phone**
Progress can be kept if you use **Sign in with Apple** (Settings → Account) on the old device, then sign in with the same Apple ID after reinstalling or on a new iPhone.

Play on a guest save that is not linked to Apple may not come back after you delete the app. Include your **Player ID** (Settings → Account → Copy) if you email us about a missing save.

**Purchases**
Rainbow coin packs and the 30 Days Pass are sold through Apple. We do not see your card details. For billing or refunds, contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing) and mention Tokenova.

**Ads**
Rewarded ads are optional. There are no forced interstitial ads.

**Sound / haptics**
Settings → Sound.
