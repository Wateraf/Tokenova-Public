# Tokenova Support

Contact: open a support request on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Wateraf/Tokenova-Public/issues/new) (no GitHub account needed to read replies on a ticket you already opened from a logged-in browser). You can also write an App Store review for Tokenova.

## Ask a question

1. [New support request](https://github.com/Wateraf/Tokenova-Public/issues/new)
2. In the app: **Settings → About → Support**
3. App Store: Tokenova → **Write a Review**

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple: [Apple billing support](https://support.apple.com/billing). Include your Player ID (Settings → Account → Copy).

## Quick help

| Topic | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Drop coins** | Center star or spacebar. Aim with the side buttons or A / D. |
| **Tickets** | D / R / A +5, G / N +30, O +50. Bottom miss +1. JACKPOT pays the board. |
| **Exchange** | 15 tickets → 1 gold coin. Gold coins cap at 1000. Rainbow coins are uncapped. |
| **Daily / timed** | Gift icon. Login once per local day. Timed claims every 10 minutes. |
| **Ads** | Optional rewarded ads only. No forced interstitials. |
| **30 Days Pass** | Daily claim while active. Lasts 30 days. Does not auto-renew. |
| **Sound / haptics** | Settings → Music / SFX sliders and Vibration |
